What this does not include

  • Capital gains tax: the portfolio is not sold. If it were sold in 2036, UK CGT at 24% would widen the gap to about €10.0M.
  • Inheritance tax, wealth taxes and the UK's post-departure rules, which depend on each family's history.
  • Cost of living and property in Monaco, and the conditions to obtain residence there.
  • Exchange rates and investment risk — returns are kept identical on purpose.
